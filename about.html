About NYEI

The origin, mission and the movement behind the initiative.

Why We Exist

Because Geography Should Not Decide Destiny.

India produces millions of students every year, hardworking, intelligent and full of potential. But talent alone does not determine outcomes. Access does. The right conversation, the right mentor, the right moment of exposure, these are the things that separate ambition from direction.

A student in a village school and a student in a metro city may carry the same intelligence. But one grew up hearing stories of IAS officers, startup founders and research scientists. The other didn't. NYEI exists to close that distance.

"We do not believe that the solution to India's talent gap is to find more talent. The talent is already there. The solution is to build better bridges between that talent and the institutions that can shape it."

Founding Convenor, NYEI

Our Mission

To Ensure No Young Indian Misses the Future Because of Where They Were Born.

"To create structured, repeatable and institution-led programs that ensure more young people in India, and farmers in India's villages, have access to the kinds of experiences, knowledge and connections that shape ambition, direction and confidence."
What We Believe

Three Beliefs That Drive Everything We Do.

📍
Exposure should not depend on geography.

A student in rural Maharashtra deserves the same quality of exposure as a student in Pune or Mumbai. Distance is not a reason. It is a problem we are solving.

🌾
AI belongs to every Indian, not just tech cities.

The farmer who learns to use AI is as important as the engineer who builds it. India's AI revolution will only be complete when it reaches every field, every classroom and every village.

🚀
The youth of today will build Bharat 2048.

We are not building for next year. We are building for the India that exists twenty years from now, and we believe the students we work with today will be the ones who shape it.
