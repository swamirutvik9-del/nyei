National Youth Exposure Initiative

India's Youth Deserves More Than a Classroom.

We are building a generation of thinkers, doers and leaders, from every corner of Bharat.

EST. 2025

Every young person in India has the capacity to grow. But not everyone gets to see what growth looks like up close. NYEI exists to change that through free AI workshops, real mentor connections and a community of high-standard youth that holds each other up.

The Exposure Gap

Talent is Universal.
Exposure Should Be.

The difference between a student who aims for IIT and one who doesn't often isn't intelligence, it's the conversation they had with someone who went there. The farmer who doubled his income with AI and the one who didn't often had the same land, one just knew the tool existed.

NYEI is that conversation. NYEI is that knowledge.
