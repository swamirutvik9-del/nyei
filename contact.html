For Students & Youth

Become a Member of NYEI.

NYEI membership is for college-going students and young people who want to be part of something real. Not just attend events, but contribute, lead, grow and build this movement with us.

What members get
✓ Access to Disha Sessions: 1-on-1 guidance from industry leaders and officials
✓ A community of high-standard, driven youth across India
✓ Real leadership roles, run your campus chapter
✓ Be part of India's AI awareness movement from the ground floor
What we expect
! Genuine commitment, not just a membership card
! Show up to weekly chapter meets
! Contribute to at least one session or initiative per month

We review every application personally. You will hear from us within 5 working days.
